# Contributor License Agreement (CLA)

Thank you for contributing to **What Is AI?**

This project is maintained by **Pro Technologies, MB** (“we”, “us”), operating as Pro AI Assistant. Contributions are accepted only under this CLA.

There are two forms:

1. **Individual CLA** — you own the contribution personally  
2. **Entity CLA** — your employer or organization owns the contribution  

## How to sign

**Individuals:** on your pull request, comment exactly:

```text
I have read the CLA Document and I hereby sign the CLA
```

The CLA GitHub Action records your GitHub username (signatures are stored on the `cla-signatures` branch). Re-comment `recheck` if the check did not update.

**Entities:** have an authorized person email **info@proaiassistant.com** with the entity name, your GitHub username(s), and confirmation that you agree to the Entity CLA below. Do not merge entity-owned work until we confirm.
